## About
I'm a passionate offensive cybersecurity student, learning and practicing daily. Focused on practical exploitation rather than simple theory.  

I write tooling with enphasis toward **web applications, APIs and reconnaissance**, the part of the job most people rush. My approach may be boring: enumerate deeply, map the attack surface properly, keep notes, make sure you got everything. But good recon is what makes the exploitation look clever.

I solve machines on Hack The Box with my team, [Blumesec](https://app.hackthebox.com/public/teams/9882), write up every single one (releasing when retired), and moderate Blumesec's cybersecurity Discord community. Currently ranked **#45 in Spain** on HTB, and held a spot in the national **top 100 for four consecutive months**.
